# Canonical Research Objective

**Status:** parked.

Study adaptive forecast-optimal trend estimation in which difference order,
finite-memory window, and smoothness may change with forecast horizon and local
time-series state:

\[
\Theta^\star_{T,h}
=
(d^\star_{T,h},L^\star_{T,h},S^\star_{T,h})
=
G(h,X_T,\mathcal C).
\]

The core questions are:

1. which mechanisms make the optimal configuration vary;
2. how quickly an adaptive procedure responds to regime changes;
3. whether adaptation improves untouched future forecasts relative to fixed
   configurations.

The persistence/AR(1) experiment is a mechanism study, not the definition of
the paper.

This paper is parked until the numerical smoothness-selection paper is
finished.
