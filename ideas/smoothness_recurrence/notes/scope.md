# Scope and Boundaries

## This paper owns

- the comparative financial experiment;
- chronological validation/test design across competing trend models;
- frozen-origin forecast paths;
- recurrence/first-passage definitions;
- censoring and survival summaries;
- cross-method and cross-asset interpretation;
- dependence-aware uncertainty for overlapping origins.

## Candidate competitors

Keep the comparison compact:

1. forecast-optimal penalized trend;
2. GCV-selected penalized trend;
3. likelihood/state-space trend;
4. AR(\(p\)) or ARIMA;
5. penalized trend + AR residual;
6. no-change/random walk.

Only add another model if it represents a clearly distinct literature-backed
forecasting principle.

## This paper does not own

- the adaptive stationary-point search in \(S\);
- epsilon-spacing as a numerical-method contribution;
- log-\(\lambda\) versus \(S\)-domain numerical benchmarking;
- regime-adaptive \((d,L,S)\) selection;
- a claim that returning to a forecast trend proves mean reversion;
- trading or portfolio conclusions.

## Dependency

This paper should resume only after the numerical paper has frozen the
forecast-optimal smoothness routine. Until then it is parked.
