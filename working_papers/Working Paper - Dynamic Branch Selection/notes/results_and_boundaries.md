Original checkpoint files and paper artifacts are preserved verbatim
where possible; keep their sample definitions, target scales and
frozen selections distinct. These numbers are **historical**
and do not certify generalized future superiority.

**Separate unrun numerical tracking benchmark:**
\`checkpoints/NUMERICAL_TRACKING_CP05_UNRUN.md\` proposes controlled
matching ground truths and **must not** be presented as executed.

The statistical challenge is not a larger matrix alone. It is the
assumption that minima can be correctly matched and their histories
contain forecasting information beyond simple historical loss aggregation.
