# Independent numerical research objective

Given a differentiable multimodal forecast-loss surface
\(F(S)\) over compact normalized smoothness \(S\in[0,1]\),
develop a numerically reliable and computationally economical
procedure to identify its stationary points, classify local
minima, evaluate the exact endpoints, and select a minimum.

Research includes the analytic resolvent derivatives,
the chain rule from penalty \(\lambda\) to \(S\), robustness
to narrow endpoint valleys, root completeness where
exact rationalization makes a Sturm procedure applicable,
and comparison against dense-grid references.

**Claims:** empirical recovery percentages for the frozen
adaptive search do not certify arbitrary real-data
global optimum discovery; Sturm completeness applies only
to the exact rationalized polynomial instance.
No multi-origin branch matching or forecast-policy mapping
is part of this paper's research objective.
