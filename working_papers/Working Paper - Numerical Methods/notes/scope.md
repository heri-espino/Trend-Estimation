# Numerical Methods scope

**Included:** one-surface multimodal stationary-point recovery,
derivative-based adaptive search, exact endpoints,
short rational Sturm root isolation, global candidate
ranking and evaluation complexity.

**Not included:** multi-origin minimum branches,
cross-time matching, or using historical trajectories
to select forecasting smoothness.

The exact numerical objective is forecast-validation
MSE for a declared finite-difference penalty and fixed
polynomial continuation, although algorithms may be
studied on controlled smooth benchmarks as well.
