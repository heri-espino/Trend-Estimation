# Numerical Methods roadmap

1. Audit objective and derivative numerical equivalence
   across reference implementations and exact endpoints.
2. Compare adaptive root searches against dense-grid,
   interval refinements and reliable numerical baselines.
3. Evaluate completeness and objective ranking on small
   rational instances for Sturm isolation.
4. Benchmark throughput, numerical accuracy and possible
   narrow extrema under fixed memory/runtime budgets.
5. Preserve frozen reference results; regenerate the
   manuscript only after choosing final algorithms.

This working paper studies numerical root recovery and
objective minimization independently of temporal minima.
