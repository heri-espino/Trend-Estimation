# Research handoff — Numerical Methods

Independent active working paper on numerical optimization
of potentially multimodal forecast-loss surfaces F(S) for
normalized PLS smoothness.

Scope: analytic derivatives, interval adaptivity, root brackets,
Brent refinement, classification, endpoint inclusion,
small-instance rational polynomial/Sturm root isolation,
objective-ranking precision and computational benchmarks.

Do NOT attribute temporal branch tracking, psi/phi selectors,
or cross-origin correspondence to this manuscript.
The historical benchmark and original solver code remain frozen
under experiments/numerical_smoothness_selection and results/.
The 240/240, 2105/2105 and 473/473 comparisons are empirical
dense-reference matches, not a completeness theorem.

The current manuscript main.tex is a working draft and will
need final numerical/LaTeX verification.
