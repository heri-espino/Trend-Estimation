\section{Model and prediction rule}
For $1\le d<L$, let $D_d$ be the consecutive order-$d$ difference
matrix and $Q_d=D_d^\top D_d$. With the mean difference $\mu$
fixed, the least-squares estimator is
\[
\widehat\tau_{\lambda,\mu}
=(I_L+\lambda Q_d)^{-1}
(x+\lambda\mu D_d^\top\mathbf1).
\]
The full affine continuation operator, including future difference
mean, will be formally specified and checked against the historical
literature before the final draft.
